NGIAB and CIROH Cyberinfrastructure Power Flash Flood Research at the 2026 Summer Institute

Ten graduate students across three teams (Don't Runoff!, FlashCast, and The Post-Processors) spent seven weeks at the 2026 Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute advancing quantitative precipitation inputs into the NextGen Framework, using NextGen In A Box (NGIAB) and NSF ACCESS Jetstream2 allocations provided through CIROH cyberinfrastructure. Read more in the blog post.

blog

CIROH Flood Inundation Mapping (FIM) Community Ecosystem: A Large-Scale Operational Flood Modeling-to-Evaluation Framework

The University of Alabama's Surface Dynamics Modeling Lab (SDML), with CIROH support, has built the CIROH FIM Community Ecosystem: an interconnected, open-source, cloud-native suite of Python tools spanning flood inundation modeling, simulation, benchmarking, and evaluation at national scale. Read more in the blog post.

FIM

Community FIM Ecosystem

Community FIM Ecosystem documentation has been newly added to the CIROH Hub. It offers a flood modeling-to-evaluation framework for flood inundation modeling, simulation, benchmarking, and evaluation.

Additonally, a new research testbed replicating the look of water.noaa.gov is now live at nwps.ciroh.org, providing a sandbox for experimenting with the National Water Prediction Service (NWPS) interface.

NGIAB

NGIAB-CloudInfra: 46,000 Docker Pulls!

The awiciroh/ciroh-ngen-image Docker image has surpassed 46,000 pulls! Learn more at ngiab.ciroh.org.

This release updates the model and build documentation, improves the model integration form, upgrades dHBV2.0 packages with corrected weights/config paths, and moves CASAM's van Genuchten parameters into shared static datasets. It also adds a new on-premise IT request issue template (replacing the previous On-Premise Request Form) and automates a monthly OWP master image build. Pinned to ngen commit 61c1400 and t-route commit f39b8d4.
